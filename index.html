What am I responsible for?

Guests are responsible for any damages, penalties, missing items, excessive cleaning or building policy violations during their stay. The security deposit may be forfeited for, but not limited to:

  • Permanent or hard-to-remove stains on linens, towels, sofa, rugs or upholstery
  • Broken furniture, décor, mirrors, appliances or kitchenware
  • Burns, scratches, dents or cracks on walls, floors, countertops, appliances or fixtures
  • Missing towels, kitchenware, remotes, keys or other provided items
  • Indoor smoking or vaping needing odor removal or deep cleaning
  • Excessive cleaning from spills, stains, trash or improper disposal
  • Building fines from rule violations
  • Damage caused by unauthorized guests or parties
